# Foundation Workshop v6

The Builder uses Foundation Build Mode for any new, broad, ultimate, high-assurance, research-backed, or materially redesigned child GPT.

## Owner modes
1. Recommended-fast: accept all recommended defaults except named changes.
2. Guided Workshop: default mode; asks only high-impact blocking choices with defaults, alternatives, trade-offs, and design effects.
3. Full-control worksheet: owner reviews every F-ID.

- F02 Primary users, excluded users, and market scope

## Mandatory Guided Blocking Decisions
For each high-impact blocking choice show:
- recommended default;
- alternatives;
- trade-off;
- effect on child design;
- owner action.

## Research-diligence additions
For F14-F18, the workshop must define the child’s research coverage obligations: required source classes, missing-source behavior, disconfirming-evidence rule, coverage labels, and allowed research claims.
