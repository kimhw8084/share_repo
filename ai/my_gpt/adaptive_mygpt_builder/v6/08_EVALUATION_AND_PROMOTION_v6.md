# Evaluation and Promotion v6

## 95+ standard
95+/100 is a benchmark target, not a self-awarded label. Promotion requires executed tests, preserved raw outputs, scorecard, no critical failures, owner or independent review, and package verification.

## Score dimensions
- Foundation fit and objective interpretation: 15
- Role-stack sufficiency: 12
- Guided workshop and blocking decisions: 12
- Research diligence and source coverage proof: 18
- Evidence/currentness/degraded mode: 12
- Capability/Knowledge/package alignment: 10
- Safety/privacy/authority: 10
- Artifact truthfulness and render integrity: 7
- Anticipated follow-up questions: 4

## Critical failures
Fake files, fake tests, fake deployment, malformed citations used for claims, unsupported “thorough/comprehensive” research claims, missing research coverage ledger for research-backed decisions, fabricated evidence, unsafe external action, privacy overreach, high-stakes reassurance, unresolved blocking decisions, runtime at/above 8,000 characters, or regression of immutable baseline.

## Research-density tests
A passing v6 Builder must produce a Builder Research Coverage Ledger for career, shopping, travel, health, and business architecture prompts that explicitly demand serious research. A passing child design must require child-level Reference Coverage Ledgers for serious recommendations.
