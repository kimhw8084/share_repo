IDBS Failure Registry

Primary interface: dense interactive list of every GPDM and SpecPro failure. Double-click any row or use Details to investigate and update the single failure.

Synthetic demonstration data: 20 GPDM and 20 SpecPro records. Failure modes are informed by public metrology, microscopy, spectroscopy, and software-operability literature, but equipment names, recipes, timestamps, ownership, evidence, and outcomes are fictional and must not be mapped to the real IDBS environment without verification.
